Atami Treasure Hunt · Paper version

No app? No problem.

Use this only when Ariff says the app is down. Everything goes through WhatsApp.

The basics

  1. 90 minutes. Starts when Ariff sends START, ends at TIME’S UP.
  2. Send everything to Ariff in a private WhatsApp chat: __________________. Begin with your group and game, e.g. “Group A – Game 2”.
  3. A game counts when Ariff replies ✅. Play the 7 games in any order. First group to finish all 7 gets +15.
  4. Stay together. Emergency: 119 (ambulance) or 110 (police), then tell Ariff.

The 7 games

1
Photo bingo

Ariff sends your bingo photos. Go to each spot and send a team selfie there. Finish every spot.

10 + 1 per photo
2
Copy the pose

Ariff sends a place and a pose. Go there, copy the pose as a team, send one photo.

10 · +5 best pose
3
Atami quiz

Find the landmark in Ariff’s photo and send a team selfie. Then Ariff calls your leader: 15 questions, 10 seconds each. Get 10 right to pass, or try again with new questions.

10 + 2 per right answer
4
Buy it, try it

Buy anything. Sell it to Tim and Ellice and video your pitch. Send the video to Ariff. The bosses score it 1 to 10.

10 + 2 × score
5
Look around you

Answer the 5 questions on page 2 on the walk from Area 3 to Area 4. Send all answers in one message, once.

10 + 2 per right answer
6
Know your colleagues

Ariff calls your leader and asks 25 questions about people in the other groups.

10 + 2 per right answer
7
Find the boss

In your “Find the boss” WhatsApp group, ask Tim and Ellice yes/no questions (2 each time they move). Find them and send a funny team photo with them.

20 · +5 funniest

Game 5 · Look around you

1. Something that is red. Name the object.
2. Name a shop or cafe sign you passed.
3. How many pedestrian crossings did you cross?
4. Something white and floating.
5. The last shop you passed before Area 4.
