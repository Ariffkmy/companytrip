Same 7 games, same rules, same points as the app. Photos, videos, answers and boss questions go by WhatsApp instead.

When to use this manual: only when the Game Master tells you the hunt has switched to offline mode (the app or server is down, or the app keeps crashing). Until then, keep playing in the app.

The clock

  • 90 minutes. It starts when Ariff sends “START” on WhatsApp and stops when he sends “TIME’S UP”. Anything sent after that doesn’t count.
  • You can finish early: send “[Group X] FINISHED” to Ariff.
  • If you switched to offline mid-hunt, the time already used still counts. Stamps you already collected in the app still count too; Ariff has them.

How to send things

Start every message with your group and game number, so Ariff can file it fast:

[Group A] Game 2 – Copy the pose (then attach the photo)
  • Ariff replies ✅ Stamp N when a game is accepted. No ✅ means not collected yet, so tick your stamp card (last page) only after the ✅.
  • The leader sends, except photo bingo and the pitch video, which anyone in the group can send.
  • Ariff goes by the WhatsApp time on each message.

Order & area

  • Play the games in any order. Skipping is allowed, but a game you never play scores nothing.
  • Stay inside the marked areas on the map. No route to follow.
  • Everyone goes to every spot together. Nobody goes off alone.

Safety

  • Count heads before you move on. Watch for cars and bikes on narrow streets.
  • Atami is hilly: walk, don’t run, on slopes and stairs. Drink water.
  • Emergency: 119 (ambulance, fire) or 110 (police), then tell Ariff.
  • No signal at all? Don’t wander looking for it. Go back to the start point together; the committee will meet you there.

Photo bingo

Find every spot on your card and take a team selfie there.

How to play

  1. Ariff sends your group its bingo card: one photo per tile, numbered. Each tile is a photo of something around the hunt area.
  2. Find the spot in a tile’s photo. The whole team goes there together.
  3. Take a selfie of the whole team at that spot.
  4. Anyone in the group sends it to Ariff, with the tile number.
  5. Do the same for every tile. When all tiles are sent, the leader sends “Collect bingo”.

Send it like this

[Group B] Game 1 – Tile 4 (attach the selfie) [Group B] Game 1 – Collect bingo
Who: anyone in the group can send a tile, from their own phone. Only the leader sends “Collect bingo”.

Scoring

10 + 1 per photo
  • 10 for the stamp.
  • 1 for every correct photo.
  • The stamp needs every tile on your card. A part-filled card scores nothing, so finish it.

Rules

  • The spot in your selfie must match the tile.
  • Everyone in the group must be in the selfie.
  • You can leave the card part-filled, play other games and come back to it.
  • Sent the wrong photo? Send the right one with the same tile number; Ariff keeps the latest.

Copy the pose

Go to the place in the first photo, then copy the pose in the second.

How to play

  1. Ariff sends your leader two photos: WHERE (the place) and POSE (the pose to copy). If there’s a Google Maps link for the place, he sends that too.
  2. Go to the place in the WHERE photo, together.
  3. Copy the pose in the POSE photo, the whole team.
  4. Take one group photo and the leader sends it to Ariff.

Send it like this

[Group C] Game 2 – Copy the pose (attach the photo)
Who: only the group leader takes and sends the pose photo.

Scoring

10 · +5 best pose
  • 10 for the stamp.
  • +5 if the committee judges yours the best pose photo of all groups (picked at the finish).

Rules

  • Everyone in the frame. Ask a passer-by to hold the phone if you have to.
  • It must be taken at the place in the WHERE photo.
  • One photo per group. If you send a retake, Ariff uses the latest.

Atami quiz

Find the landmark, take a selfie, then answer the quiz: How well do you know Atami?

Part A · The selfie

  1. Ariff sends your leader the WHERE photo of a landmark. Work out where it is and go there.
  2. Take a team selfie with everyone in it and the landmark behind you. The leader sends it to Ariff.
  3. When Ariff replies “Quiz unlocked”, go on to Part B.
[Group D] Game 3 – Selfie (attach the selfie)

Part B · The quiz (WhatsApp call)

  1. The leader messages “[Group D] Game 3 – Ready for the quiz”. Ariff calls the leader on WhatsApp. Put it on speaker.
  2. Ariff reads 15 questions, one at a time, with the answer choices.
  3. You have 10 seconds per question. The leader says the team’s answer. Ariff tells you right or wrong.
  4. Out of time on a question? The round is over: start again from question 1 with new questions.
  5. Get at least 10 of 15 right to pass. Fall short (or can’t reach 10 any more) and you get 15 new questions to try again.
  6. Pass, and Ariff replies ✅ Stamp 3.
Who: only the group leader sends the selfie and answers the questions. Decide together first.

Scoring

10 + 2 per right answer
  • 10 for the stamp.
  • 2 for every right answer in your passing round.
  • Pass mark: 10 of 15. So a pass is worth at least 30.

Rules

  • Answer as a team.
  • No searching, no asking anyone outside the team, no AI.
  • Call dropped? Message Ariff; he calls back and carries on from the same question.
  • If Ariff is on another group’s call, wait for his call back. He goes in the order your “Ready” messages arrived.

Buy it, try it

Buy anything you like, then sell it to the bosses.

How to play

  1. Buy anything you like. Any amount.
  2. Take what you bought to Tim and Ellice when you find them (see game 7), and convince them to buy it from you.
  3. Anyone in the group records the pitch as a video (up to 2 minutes) and sends it to Ariff.
  4. The boss who heard your pitch rates how convincing it was, 1 to 10, and sends the rating to Ariff on WhatsApp.
  5. When Ariff has both the video and the rating, he replies ✅ Stamp 4 with your rating.

Send it like this

[Group A] Game 4 – Pitch video (attach the video)
Who: anyone in the group can record and send the video. The rating comes from the bosses, not from you.

Scoring

10 + 2 × rating
  • 10 for the stamp.
  • 2 for every mark of the bosses’ 1–10 rating.
  • Up to 30.

Rules

  • The stamp needs the video and the rating. No video, no stamp.
  • Video too big for WhatsApp? Record a shorter one, or send it as a document.
  • You can pitch while playing game 7, since you’re with the bosses anyway.

Look around you

Notice everything on the walk: the answers are along the way, not on the internet.

How to play

  1. Play it on the walk from Area 3 to Area 4. Every answer is somewhere along that stretch: shop signs, things in windows, what’s on the street and above it.
  2. Split the questions between you before you set off. Keep your eyes up: once you reach Area 4 you can’t walk back to check.
  3. Work out the answers together and write them below.
  4. The leader sends all answers in one message. You only send once, so check them first.

Send it like this

[Group B] Game 5 – Look around you 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. …
Who: only the group leader sends the answers. Work them out together.

Scoring

10 + 2 per right answer
  • 10 for the stamp.
  • 2 for every right answer. The committee marks them.
  • Up to 20.

Rules

  • Phones down: none of this is on the internet.
  • One answer per question.
  • Only your first answer message counts.

The questions

1. Something that is red. Name the object.
2. Name a shop or cafe sign you passed on the way.
3. How many pedestrian crossings did you cross between Area 3 and Area 4?
4. Something white and floating.
5. The last shop you passed before reaching Area 4. Name it.

Do you know your colleagues?

Questions about the people in the other groups, all mixed up.

How to play

  1. The leader messages “[Group C] Game 6 – Ready”. Ariff calls the leader on WhatsApp. Put it on speaker so the whole group hears.
  2. Ariff reads each question with its answer choices. The questions are about people in the other groups, all mixed up.
  3. Decide together. The leader says the answer. Ariff tells you right or wrong; if it’s wrong, he tells you the right one.
  4. Answer all 25 questions. After the last one, Ariff replies ✅ Stamp 6 with your score.
Who: only the group leader answers. Everyone else listens on speaker and helps decide.

Scoring

10 + 2 per right answer
  • 10 for the stamp.
  • 2 for every right answer.
  • There’s no minimum score. Unlike the Atami quiz you can’t fail this one: the stamp is yours after the last question.

Rules

  • No asking the people the questions are about.
  • No messaging other groups for answers.
  • Call dropped? Message Ariff; he carries on from the same question.

Find the boss

Tim and Ellice are hiding in the hunt area. Ask yes/no questions, then find them.

How to play

  1. Your group has its own “Find the boss – Group X” WhatsApp group with Tim, Ellice and Ariff. Other groups can’t see it.
  2. The leader asks the bosses questions there. They only answer Yes or No, truthfully.
  3. Ask one question at a time: wait for the answer before the next.
  4. Your group gets only 2 questions. When the bosses move to a new spot, they post “🚶 We’re moving” and you get 2 more.
  5. Use the answers to work out where they are, and go find them.
  6. Take one funny-pose photo of the whole team with Tim and Ellice. The leader sends it to Ariff.

Send it like this

In “Find the boss – Group D”: Are you near the water? To Ariff: [Group D] Game 7 – Boss photo (attach the photo)
Who: only the group leader asks the bosses and sends the photo. Everyone in the group sees the answers.

Scoring

20 · +5 funniest
  • 20 for the selfie with Tim and Ellice (this game has no separate 10-point stamp).
  • +5 if the committee judges yours the funniest group photo (picked at the finish).

Rules

  • Yes/no questions only. “Where are you?” gets no answer, and still uses one of your 2.
  • No hints from the bosses, in the chat or in person.
  • Your whole team must be in the photo, with both bosses.
  • While you’re with them, do your Buy it, try it pitch (game 4).
