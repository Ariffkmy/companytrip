No app? No problem.

Use this only when Ariff says the app is down. Every game is played through WhatsApp with the Game Master.

The basics

  1. 90 minutes. Starts when Ariff sends START, ends at TIME’S UP. Anything sent after TIME’S UP doesn’t count.
  2. Game Master: Ariff. Send everything to him in a private WhatsApp chat: __________________.
  3. Caption every message with its code (table below). No code, no points.
  4. A game counts when Ariff replies ✅. Play the 7 games in any order. First group to finish all 7 gets +15.
  5. Stay together. The whole group goes to every spot. Emergency: 119 (ambulance) or 110 (police), then tell Ariff.

The 7 games, step by step

1
Photo bingo
  1. Ariff sends your group’s bingo photos, numbered 1, 2, 3 …
  2. Find each spot and take a team selfie there, everyone in the shot.
  3. Send each selfie as you take it, captioned with its number: GroupA_photobingo_1.
  4. Ariff replies ✅ when every spot is done.
10 + 1 per photo
2
Copy the pose
  1. Ariff sends a place and a pose photo.
  2. Go to that place and copy the pose as a whole group.
  3. Send one photo: GroupA_copythepose.
10 · +5 best pose
3
Atami quiz
  1. Ariff sends a landmark photo. Find it and send a team selfie: GroupA_atamiquiz_landmark.
  2. Ariff replies with 20 question numbers from the quiz pages at the back of this manual.
  3. You have 5 minutes. Send all 20 answers in one message: GroupA_atamiquiz_round1, then one line each, e.g. 12 B.
  4. 10 or more right is a pass. Fewer than 10? Ariff sends 20 new numbers: reply GroupA_atamiquiz_round2. No searching online.
10 + 2 per right answer
4
Buy it, try it
  1. Buy anything from a shop in Atami.
  2. Find Tim and Ellice and sell it to them. Record your pitch as a video.
  3. Send the video: GroupA_buyittryit. The bosses score it 1 to 10 and tell Ariff.
10 + 2 × score
5
Look around you
  1. On the walk from Area 3 to Area 4, answer the 5 questions on the next page.
  2. Send all 5 answers in one message, once: GroupA_lookaround, then lines 1 to 5. Only your first message counts.
10 + 2 per right answer
6
Know your colleagues
  1. When your group is together and somewhere quiet, text GroupA_colleagues_ready.
  2. Ariff calls your leader and asks 25 questions about people in the other groups. Answer together.
10 + 2 per right answer
7
Find the boss
  1. In your “Find the boss – Group A” WhatsApp group, ask Tim and Ellice yes/no questions. You get 2 questions each time they post “🚶 We’re moving”.
  2. Find them and take a funny team photo with both of them.
  3. Send it to Ariff: GroupA_findtheboss.
20 · +5 funniest
