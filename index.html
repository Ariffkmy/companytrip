Halal Plan & Prayer Facilities

  • Cinta Jawa Café (Yokohama) - Indonesian halal-certified
  • Khazana Minatomirai - halal-certified meat (India)
  • AFURI Yokohama Joinus - halal-certified ramen (small shop)

🟡 Tier 2 — Pork-Free / Lard-Free / Alcohol-Free

  • Kamatsuru (Atami) - pork-free kitchen + halal chef certification, official Shizuoka portal
  • Tensho / Osakana Shokudo Hanare / Nagisa (Atami) - seafood upon request without pork/sake/mirin
  • SARIO Heichinsaryoo (Yokohama) - Muslim-friendly menu since 2018
  • Kondo (Kamakura) - halal seasoning, vegetarian OK

⚠️ Written request + confirmation required: sake/mirin is used in many Japanese dishes. Phrase: 豚肉・ラード・アルコール（料理酒・みりん）不使用でお願いします

🔵 Muslim-Friendly (Muslim owner/staff, separate kitchen)

  • Malay Asian Cuisine (Yokohama) - 🇲🇾 Muslim owner & chef, Malay-speaking staff, separate kitchen
  • Kissho Yokohama - halal-friendly chain
  • EGE Turkish (Kamakura) · Vee Sweets Café (plant-based)
  • Hachinoki (Kamakura) - fully vegetarian

📿 Prayer Spaces

  • ATAMI Sekaie - prayer space + prayer mat + Quran + qibla compass ✅
  • Kamatsuru (Atami) - prayer space available (Shizuoka portal)
  • Landmark Tower Yokohama - prayer room (check opening hours)
  • World Porters Yokohama - prayer space inside the mall
  • Atami Tourist Information Center — prayer space
  • Official Kanagawa portal (full list): trip.pref.kanagawa.jp/muslim-tourism

❌ Avoid (confirmed not halal)

  • Chinatown dinner (Peking Hanten, Ryushoki, etc.) - pork is a staple in Chinese cooking; visit for photos/shopping only
  • Ie-kei ramen & Noge/Isezakai izakaya - use pork/sake
  • Shirasu donburi & regular seafood stalls - not certified

🌦️ October Weather

15-22°C - comfortable, cool at night. Bring a jacket. Autumn colors start appearing. Fireworks night on 25 Oct: warm clothes + top up Suica early (stations get crowded after 9 PM).

📅 Early Booking (Required!)

  • Atami ryokan on 25 Oct - fireworks night, will definitely fill up. For halal: ATAMI Sekaie.
  • 30-person venues - Khazana / Cinta Jawa (charter) / Sekaie - reserve immediately
  • Hachinoki shojin lunch & Cup Noodles Museum (make-your-own cup noodle session) - reserve

📌 Tips for 30 pax

  • AirAsia group booking - 10+ pax has a group sales team; one transaction, easier management for 30 tickets + potentially better pricing.
  • Return red-eye (23:50) = full Monday 26 Oct free - daytime activities, evening shinkansen, red-eye home. But arrival in KUL is Tuesday 6:15 AM - consider post-trip leave.

  • Book as soon as possible - prices rise closer to the date and during the Deepavali season (late Oct).
